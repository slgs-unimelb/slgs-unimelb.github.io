# Sri Lankan Graduates’ Society - University of Melbourne

The official website of the Sri Lankan Graduates’ Society (SLGS) at the University of Melbourne, Australia.

## About SLGS

The Sri Lankan Graduates’ Society (SLGS) is a postgraduate society affiliated with the Graduate Student Association (GSA) and the University of Melbourne Student Union (UMSU) at the University of Melbourne. The society represents and supports Sri Lankan postgraduate students while promoting Sri Lankan culture and heritage within the university’s multicultural community.

## Mission

To represent the Sri Lankan graduate student community at the University of Melbourne by:

- Supporting incoming and current students
- Promoting Sri Lankan culture and values
- Building social and cultural connections
- Encouraging leadership development and student growth
- Enhancing academic and professional life for members

## Vision

To foster a safe, inclusive, and inspiring environment for Sri Lankan graduate students at the University of Melbourne.

## What We Do

SLGS organizes a wide range of academic, social, and cultural activities throughout the year, including:

- Sri Lankan cultural celebrations
- Community gatherings and networking events
- Welfare and advocacy support for students
- Charity and promotional initiatives
- Professional and leadership development activities

The society also contributes to multicultural events across the University of Melbourne community.

## Community

SLGS is home to a diverse network of Sri Lankan postgraduate students from multiple faculties and disciplines across the University of Melbourne. The society aims to create a welcoming “home away from home” for students living and studying in Australia.

## Membership

SLGS membership is available through the University of Melbourne Student Union (UMSU). Members gain access to events, networking opportunities, cultural activities, and community support initiatives.
