Who We Are

The Sri Lankan Graduates’ Society (SLGS) is a postgraduate society affiliated to the Graduate Student Association (GSA) at the University of Melbourne, Australia.

Our Achievements →

Our Vision

To foster a safe and inclusive environment and to provide a memorable and inspirational experience to Sri Lankan graduate students at the University of Melbourne.

Our Mission

To represent the Sri Lankan graduate student cohort of University of Melbourne by providing support to incoming and current students, building social-cultural bonds, facilitating leadership development, and encouraging student growth.

What We Offer

SLGS works with the University of Melbourne to provide services that uplift academic, social, and professional life.

Advocacy & Welfare

Support for Sri Lankan graduate students studying at the University of Melbourne.

Cultural Activities

Introducing Sri Lankan culture and heritage to the wider multicultural university community.

Charity & Community Service

Events that aim to help both Sri Lanka and the University of Melbourne community.

Student Growth

Motivating Sri Lankan students to pursue undergraduate and postgraduate studies at the University of Melbourne.

Professional & Academic Networks

Building meaningful connections across faculties through events and collaboration.

Leadership Development

Opportunities to contribute, lead initiatives, and grow within a supportive community.

How to Join

SLGS is affiliated with the University of Melbourne Student Union (UMSU). Members are required to renew membership on the UMSU website.

Join in 4 Steps

Complete your membership through the UMSU portal in just a few minutes.

Join via UMSU
  1. 1
    Open the SLGS Page on UMSU Use the Join button to start your membership.
  2. 2
    Sign In or Create a Guest Account Use your UniMelb credentials or create a Guest profile.
  3. 3
    Add Membership to Basket Confirm the SLGS membership selection before checkout.
  4. 4
    Checkout and Confirm Finish the checkout to activate your membership.

Whether you’re a postgraduate student or a member of staff, we’d love to have you with us. Come grow, connect, and celebrate together, all year round.
