Scholarships

Each year, SLGS raises funds to help Sri Lankan undergraduate students pursue their studies. As a Sri Lankan, you can give back to your motherland by joining hands with us and contributing to the Scholarship Program.

Objective

The main objective of this program is to financially assist Sri Lankan undergraduate students to pursue their academic endeavours. Our goal is to raise funds for scholarships awarded monthly for a period of one year. Currently, SLGS provides two scholarships worth Rs. 120,000, and we expect to expand the number of scholarships in the future.

The Annual Program Cycle

Funds are collected mainly from ticket sales for our Annual Gathering and Dinner Dance, and contributions are welcome throughout the year.

  1. 1
    Candidate Selection SLGS chooses the university that will receive the scholarships, and its Vice Chancellor and deans nominate 10 potential candidates. SLGS then selects 5 of them.
  2. 2
    Grant Scholarships SLGS transfers the annual scholarship funds to the university, which grants them to the selected students through its Bursar.
